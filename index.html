Our Story

WHO WE ARE? WHAT WE DO?

Lion Kithul Wood is a small family business in southern Sri Lanka, we create beautiful, sustainable housewares from kithul and coconut wood. We are committed to using sustainably sourced local materials and providing natural, durable alternatives to imported plastic items.

Products include wooden spoons, forks, chopsticks, plates, bowls, cups, trays, table mats and runners, soap dishes, storage containers, and furniture.
